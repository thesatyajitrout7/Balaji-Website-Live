Our Specialties

At Shree Balaji Nursing Home, we provide expert care across multiple specialties, ensuring comprehensive treatment with a personalized touch from our skilled medical team.

Cardiology

Diagnosis and treatment of congenital heart defects, coronary artery disease, heart failure, valvular heart disease, and electrophysiology.

Orthopedics & Joint Care

Bone fractures, arthritis treatment, joint replacement surgeries, sports injuries etc.

Gynecology & Obstetrics

Pregnancy care, normal & C-section delivery, fertility treatments, PCOS management, etc.

Dental Care

Root Canal Treatment, Crown & Bridges, Wisdom Tooth Removal, Orthodontic Fixed Wire Treatment, Ultrasonic Scaling & Polishing, etc.

Maxillofacial Surgery

Specialized care for facial injuries, jaw disorders, and complex oral conditions.

General Surgery

Laparoscopic surgery, hernia repair, appendix removal, gallbladder surgery, etc.

MD Medicine

Management of diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, infectious diseases, etc.

ENT (Ear, Nose, Throat)

Sinus treatment, tonsillectomy, hearing loss treatment, nasal surgery, etc.
